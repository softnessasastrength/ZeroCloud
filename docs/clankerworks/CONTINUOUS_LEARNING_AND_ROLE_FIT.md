# ClankerLearn: Always-Available Development and Role-Fit Recovery

Status: proposed fictional HR and learning architecture, documentation only. Additive to draft PR #13 and other unmerged ZeroCloud HR proposals.

## Principle
Difficulty in one assignment is not proof of low employee potential. Provide continuous access to learning resources and fair opportunities for role exploration, including for employees experiencing performance problems. Curiosity and transfers remain voluntary where feasible.

## Always-available access
A 24/7 learning portal should provide self-paced courses, searchable role maps, qualification prerequisites, accessible materials, training requests and scheduling. 'Available at all times' means access and ability to request support, not a guarantee of immediate instructor availability or unlimited paid hours. Actual paid training windows and staffing coverage must be explicit and equitable.

## Development before displacement
1. Identify performance concerns using task-normalized, inspectable evidence and check equipment, workflow, training, safety, accessibility and upstream constraints.
2. Discuss work experience and preferences privately, without assuming lack of motivation or requiring medical disclosure.
3. Offer current-role coaching, supported training, or an exploratory transfer pathway.
4. When feasible, schedule a supervised trial with trainee-appropriate goals and qualified supervision.
5. Assess fit across task-specific quality, safety, employee preference and sustainable performance, documenting limitations and avoiding unfair cross-role comparisons.
6. Jointly review continued coaching, reassignment, other suitable openings or other appropriate next steps. Preserve due process, appeal and applicable accommodations.

## Guardrails
- Performance coaching and intentional misconduct are distinct processes.
- Do not promise a transfer that lacks an actual opening, required qualification, safe placement or operational feasibility.
- Avoid using trial placement as a covert disciplinary assignment or as an excuse to deny legally required accommodation.
- No guaranteed immunity from legitimate discipline or employment decisions; fair investigation and documented options come first.
- Employees succeeding in their current roles retain equal access to learning and internal mobility.
- Do not create a permanent 'poor performer' label; preserve corrections, review periods and role-specific context.
- Employee records are confidential and access-controlled.

## Synthetic tests for future implementation
- Packer struggling with task pace can request inventory-control training without prior disciplinary escalation.
- Supervisor sees only authorized role-fit information, not unnecessary medical details.
- Trial assignment uses trainee-specific expectations and records actual qualifications.
- No available position results in transparent alternatives rather than a fabricated transfer.
- Employee thriving in packing can access identical learning catalog.
- Performance record can be challenged and corrected, with original decision provenance retained.

## Open decisions
Define paid training allocation, trial duration, transfer eligibility, union/employee representation, appeal timelines, cross-department staffing approvals, and separation between HR records and Career Ledger.
