# ClankerWorks: Evidence-Based Discipline, Independent Appeals and Real Open Door

Status: **proposed fictional governance and HR policy**, not adopted or legally reviewed. Additive to draft PR #13; reconcile with earlier unmerged HR, constitutional and corporate governance proposals. No employee records or proprietary employer materials.

## Core distinction: task performance is not employee worth
A worker who misses packing targets but excels at rebin demonstrates why metrics must be role-, assignment-, task-mix-, and condition-specific. Poor results in one process do not automatically establish poor overall capability or misconduct. Before a performance-based action, verify the standard, data provenance, denominator, training, equipment, task mix, safety constraints, accessibility and suitable coaching/role-fit alternatives. An alternative placement is not always available, but the inquiry and decision should be documented.

## Discipline must have a justification record
No final formal corrective action without:
1. Specific allegation, date/time range and action classification (performance, conduct, safety, etc.).
2. Identified policy and version applicable at the time.
3. Source evidence, provenance, uncertainties and relevant exculpatory/contextual evidence.
4. Employee notice, accessible opportunity to respond, and representation rights where applicable.
5. Analysis of training, equipment, role fit, accommodations where applicable, and alternatives to discipline.
6. Proportionality rationale and comparison with applicable consistent standards, with privacy-preserving review.
7. Decision maker, conflict checks, independent approvals and escalation record.
8. Written outcome, appeal instructions, deadlines, correction/remedy mechanism and retention policy.

A vague 'failed to meet expectations' statement alone is insufficient. Distinguish allegations from findings and disputed from verified facts. An audit log documents a decision; it does not automatically make that decision fair.

## Open Door with enforceable procedure
- Allow bypass of the immediate manager and confidential reporting to independent reviewers.
- Acknowledge receipt, set published response and escalation timelines, track status and explain delays.
- Screen for conflicts and recuse implicated managers; ensure reviewers can reverse findings and order remedies.
- Protect against retaliation, track subsequent adverse actions for review, and preserve lawful external reporting and employee representation rights.
- Offer accessible channels, including non-digital options, and do not require confrontation with the subject of a complaint.
- Record reasoning and disposition with strict need-to-know access; publish only privacy-preserving aggregate process metrics.

## Appeals
Independent reviewer outside original decision chain, meaningful evidence access subject to legitimate confidentiality, employee rebuttal, ability to submit new evidence, reasoned written decision and remedies such as reversal, personnel-record correction, restored pay when appropriate, retraining or new investigation. Escalation must be available if the reviewer is conflicted or misses deadlines. No automatic 'upheld' decisions or success measured solely by closure rate.

## Emergency measures
Immediate safety-related reassignment or removal can occur when necessary without prejudging fault; require prompt independent review, written rationale and a separate subsequent disciplinary determination. Follow applicable wage, employment and safety requirements.

## Proposed technical controls
- Structured case record with immutable event history plus correctable substantive records, versioned policies and access logging.
- Block finalization when required evidence/notice/review fields are absent; allow emergency safety pathway with expiration and oversight.
- Separation of duties: subject manager cannot be sole investigator, final approver and appeal adjudicator.
- Privacy controls for witnesses, complainants and protected information.
- Exportable employee case summary and documented correction/appeal outcomes.
- Independent audit of aggregate response times, overturned cases, repeat policy errors and retaliation allegations, with careful interpretation.

## Synthetic validation scenarios
1. Under-target packer with excellent rebin results receives contextual role-fit review before a performance decision.
2. Manager attempts to finalize discipline with 'bad attitude' and no specifics; system refuses finalization.
3. Employee appeals manager-originated action; independent reviewer can reverse and correct the record.
4. Complaint about a manager is routed outside that manager's chain.
5. Emergency safety removal is reviewed without being automatically classified as misconduct.
6. Unauthorized personnel cannot view confidential case details; authorized accesses are logged.
7. Missed appeal deadline triggers escalation and documented remedy.

## Open decisions
Define procedural deadlines, evidentiary thresholds by action type, employee/union representation, independent reviewer appointment and removal, remedies, record retention, anti-retaliation oversight and jurisdiction-specific legal review.
