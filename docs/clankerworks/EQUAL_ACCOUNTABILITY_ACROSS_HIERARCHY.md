# ClankerWorks: Equal Accountability Across the Hierarchy

Status: proposed fictional constitutional and HR principle. Additive to draft PR #13 and earlier unmerged leadership/HR/governance proposals. No enacted policy or legal determination.

## Constitutional principle
No employee, supervisor, manager, executive or corporate officer is exempt from the fundamental standards they enforce. Greater authority creates additional duties of care, accurate reporting, procedural fairness, escalation and accountability, never immunity.

## Equal principles, role-specific duties
Apply consistent baseline standards for honesty, safety, respectful conduct, evidence and nonretaliation. Leadership roles carry additional obligations because they can control assignments, records, pay recommendations, investigations and careers. Equal due process does not imply identical sanctions for materially different responsibilities or harm.

## Decision challenge is not misconduct
Good-faith requests for evidence, challenges to calculations, policy questions, protected complaints and appeals are not automatically insubordination. Employees may challenge a decision without repudiating the manager's general authority. Legitimate directions and immediate safety instructions remain subject to applicable policy and law.

## Enforceable controls
1. Managers are subject to the same documented allegation, evidence, response, proportionality and appeal framework as other employees.
2. A manager must not be sole investigator, adjudicator or appeal reviewer of a complaint about themselves or their own decision.
3. Escalation outside the reporting chain and independent review are available at all organizational levels, including executives.
4. Overrides of disciplinary findings require written reasons, authority, conflict checks and independent review; no invisible executive exception.
5. Misuse of authority, retaliation, falsified evidence and deliberate suppression of complaints are independently reviewable violations.
6. Outcomes are communicated appropriately while respecting confidentiality, witness protection and employee privacy.
7. Auditors can review aggregate patterns of manager misconduct findings, overturned discipline, delayed appeals and retaliation concerns.

## Synthetic acceptance tests
- Associate challenges inaccurate packing productivity calculation without automatic misconduct flag.
- Supervisor who falsifies supporting evidence is independently investigated and can lose supervisory authority.
- Complaint against senior executive routes to reviewer outside that executive's control.
- Attempt to silently override an upheld finding is blocked and logged.
- Manager accused of wrongdoing receives fair notice and an opportunity to respond, with no presumption of guilt.

## Open decisions
Independent reviewer appointment/removal, employee representation, executive oversight, appeal timelines, audit publication, sanctions matrix and legal/jurisdiction review.
