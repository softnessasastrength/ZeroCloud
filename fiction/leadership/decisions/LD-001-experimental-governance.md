# ZeroCloud Leadership Decision 001: Experimental Governance

**Status:** Accepted as working fictional canon
**Selection:** D
**Date:** 2026-11-12

## Decision
Different fictional departments or teams operate under different, explicitly documented enforcement models for ZeroCloud's original leadership principles. Fictional incidents compare outcomes across these governance regimes. No model is assumed superior in advance.

## Models
- **Model A, Advisory:** principles inform coaching and review; remedies are recommendations unless other policy applies.
- **Model B, Binding managerial code:** substantiated violations can trigger corrective action under published rules and independent fact-finding.
- **Model C, Constitutional workplace rights:** employees may invoke specified principles, independent reviewers may order narrowly defined remedies, with due process and appeal.
- **Model D, Experimental federation:** teams operate under one of the above models for the fiction experiment, while nonwaivable baseline protections apply everywhere.

## Nonwaivable baseline
Every team has access to wage-payment choices as proposed in HR drafts, nonretaliation, accessible open-door reporting, independent review requests, safety escalation, and privacy protections. Experimental governance must not be used to excuse discrimination, retaliation, wage violations, or bypass of applicable law. These are fictional policy proposals, not representations of real legal compliance.

## Assignment and evaluation
Each fictional team has a public charter specifying its assigned model, accountable decision maker, reviewer independence, appeal route, change-control process, and evaluation period. Do not change models mid-incident to predetermine a narrative outcome. Use common scenarios, consistent evidence, and documented deviations. Compare resolution time, employee agency, procedural fairness, decision quality, operational impact and unintended consequences. Distinguish simulated results from empirical evidence.

## Open questions
- Who assigns teams to governance models?
- What authority can a reviewer exercise under each model?
- How long is an evaluation cycle?
- How do employees consent or object to a model?
- Who arbitrates conflicts between team autonomy and the corporate baseline?
