# ZeroCloud small autonomous team charter

**Status:** Proposed fictional operating model, inspired by the publicly discussed small-team / two-pizza organizational concept. This document is original ZeroCloud writing, not a reproduction of Amazon internal material.

## Team design
- Favor small cross-functional teams with a clearly bounded product or service mission. Pizza is a metaphor, not a headcount certification metric.
- Assign one accountable service owner and document team decision rights.
- Allow local architecture and delivery decisions within published security, accessibility, employee-rights and interoperability constraints.
- Publish API contracts, operational dependencies, incident ownership and service runbooks.
- Provide escalation when teams conflict, without automatic veto by a more senior manager.
- Avoid making a single team the judge of complaints against its own leadership.

## Governance experiment
Create fictional teams with similar technical obligations but different leadership enforcement models:
- Team Atlas: advisory principles.
- Team Harbor: binding management code.
- Team Lantern: constitutional review rights.

These are fictional labels and experimental assignments, not actual departments or personnel. The corporate baseline applies to all three.

## Review questions
- Does small-team autonomy improve speed at the expense of oversight?
- What happens when a team's security decisions affect another team?
- Can an employee bypass their own service owner to raise concerns?
- Does a small team amplify conflicts of interest?
- When does a small team become an understaffed team?

## Editorial constraint
Do not assume the small-team concept always works. Write failures, boundary cases, staffing strain, coordination costs and contradictory viewpoints.
