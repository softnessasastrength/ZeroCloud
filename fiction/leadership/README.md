# ZeroCloud Leadership Constitution: narrative design

**Status:** Working fictional proposals, not ratified corporate canon.
**Context:** ZeroCloud is an openly fictional, open-source-first corporation.

## Creative thesis
Leadership ideals are most revealing when keeping them costs an institution something. Compare published corporate leadership frameworks as research, then write original ZeroCloud principles. Do not copy proprietary training material, internal records or lengthy verbatim published text into this public repository.

## Initial original principle candidates
1. Human consequences first: consider customers, employees and communities together.
2. Authority carries accountability: leaders answer for foreseeable and unintended harms.
3. Act decisively, document responsibly: speed is not a waiver of care.
4. Trust requires verifiable conduct: titles and slogans are not evidence.
5. Dissent remains protected: a commitment to execute does not prohibit principled challenge.
6. Investigate before assigning blame: evidence precedes disciplinary conclusions.
7. Results include how they were achieved: outcomes do not excuse misconduct.
8. Challenge authority without retaliation: escalation and independent review must work in practice.

## Narrative engine
For each principle create:
- Promise: the exact original ZeroCloud commitment.
- Cost: a fictional scenario where honoring it is inconvenient.
- Conflict: distinct departmental interpretations and voices.
- Decision: evidence, authority, consequences and review.
- Precedent: versioned policy changes and institutional memory.

## Editorial boundaries
- Satire critiques institutional incentives, not ordinary workers or learners.
- Do not fictionalize real identifiable people or private workplace records without deliberate consent and transformation.
- Real company frameworks are comparison subjects, not ZeroCloud's own words.
- Distinguish proposed, ratified, implemented and independently verified practices.
- Preserve the separate HR decision process; HR-001 remains pending.
