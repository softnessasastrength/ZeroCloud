# CASE-001: The disputed schedule

**Entirely fictional writing exercise. No real employee or employer is depicted.**

A manager changes an engineer's schedule after a dispute about documentation quality. The engineer believes the change is retaliatory; the manager says it is needed for operational coverage. The engineer invokes ZC-HR-OPEN-001 and requests an outside-manager review under ZC-HR-REVIEW-001.

## Required artifacts and voices
1. Employee statement: specific, anxious, focused on consequences and fairness.
2. Manager rationale: concise, operational, potentially defensive.
3. Intake acknowledgement: neutral, procedural, privacy-conscious.
4. Outside-manager finding: evidence-weighted, discloses limitations.
5. HR process audit: checks consistency, retaliation and accommodation concerns.
6. Appeal ruling: distinct voice and reasoning, not a rubber stamp.

## Ambiguities to preserve
- The timing of the schedule change is suspicious but not dispositive.
- Coverage may genuinely be inadequate.
- Both parties may omit relevant context.
- The independent reviewer may identify procedural unfairness without finding retaliatory intent.

## Editorial constraint
Do not write any character as a caricature or pre-decide the result. The process must be capable of ruling against either party.
