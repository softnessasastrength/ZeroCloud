# ZC-HR-OPEN-001: Enforceable open-door access

**Status:** Proposed fictional policy

An open door is a procedure, not a slogan.

1. Employee may contact HR, an independent reviewing manager, or the ombuds channel directly, including when their manager is the subject of the concern.
2. Publish at least two access routes, including an accessible written route; avoid dependence on company-controlled devices only.
3. Acknowledge concerns, provide a reference ID, explain next steps and communicate meaningful status updates.
4. Protect confidentiality to the extent possible, explain its limits, and prohibit retaliation.
5. Separate intake from final adjudication when feasible; preserve records and disclose conflicts.
6. Provide an escalation path for nonresponse, compromised intake, and alleged retaliation.
7. Distinguish ordinary disagreement, misconduct, safety concerns, discrimination and urgent threats; route each appropriately.
8. Publish anonymized aggregate metrics and policy exceptions, not identifiable case files.

## Fiction stress tests
- Employee reports the manager who controls their schedule.
- HR fails to respond and the employee escalates externally.
- Two employees submit contradictory reports.
- The person receiving a complaint has a conflict of interest.
