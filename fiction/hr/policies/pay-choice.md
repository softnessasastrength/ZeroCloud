# ZC-HR-PAY-001: Employee payment-method choice

**Status:** Proposed fictional policy
**Intent:** Employees must have a meaningful, accessible way to receive wages without being forced into a bank account, direct deposit, or payroll card.

## Draft normative rules
1. Offer paper checks as an available election, subject to verified applicable law and practical delivery arrangements.
2. Provide clear written instructions for choosing or changing the method, including an accessible offline route.
3. No retaliation, punitive fees, reduced hours, or adverse treatment solely for selecting paper payment.
4. Establish check delivery, replacement, lost-check and error-resolution procedures; maintain wage-payment deadlines.
5. Minimize sensitive banking information and limit payroll access.
6. Track requests and resolution times without publishing real employee details.

## Fiction stress tests
- Employee has no bank account.
- Check is delayed or lost during a holiday.
- Payroll vendor discourages paper checks.
- An employee changes payment methods just before payroll cutoff.

## Open research
Verify jurisdiction-specific wage payment and consent rules before representing this as legal guidance or deploying it in a real organization.
