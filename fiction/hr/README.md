# ZeroCloud fictional People Operations

**Status: Working fictional canon, not adopted law or operational HR policy.**

ZeroCloud is an openly fictional corporation. These policies explore an employer designed around employee dignity, meaningful procedural fairness and management accountability. They are creative-writing and institutional-design artifacts, not claims about an existing employer.

## Recorded proposals (2026-11-12)
- Employees may elect a **paper paycheck** rather than being compelled to use direct deposit or a payroll card. The fictional payroll office must publish a workable request and delivery process, protect employee choice, and verify any jurisdiction-specific payroll rules before any real-world implementation.
- The **open-door policy must be actionable**: employees can raise concerns without seeking the permission of the manager concerned, receive acknowledgement and a case reference, and access an escalation path without retaliation.
- A **decentralized manager-review system** lets an employee request an impartial manager outside their reporting line to review a dispute. Reviewers must disclose conflicts and recuse when necessary. No promise of neutrality is sufficient without selection rules and independent appeal.

These are founding design proposals, not yet a fully ratified HR constitution. Decision HR-001 remains unselected.

## Fiction writing objectives
Write the same incident from the employee, manager, reviewing manager, payroll, HR, and independent appeal perspectives. Keep voices and incentives distinct. Test how policy performs when staffing is limited, evidence conflicts, or retaliation is alleged.
