# CORP-002: Dual representation in the ZeroCloud Union

**Status:** Accepted working fictional canon
**Decision:** C, dual representation
**Recorded:** 2026-11-12
**Dependency:** CORP-001, cooperative federation (draft PR #7)

## Decision
The fictional ZeroCloud Union has two distinct democratic mandates:
- **Union Parliament:** directly represents employees of member companies.
- **Council of Members:** represents the autonomous member companies.

Neither institution is interchangeable with the other. Federation legislation affecting both employees and members ordinarily requires participation by both chambers under procedures still to be specified.

## EU-inspired, not EU-identical
This is an original fictional corporate constitution drawing on the general idea of dual representation in European Union governance. It is not a statement that EU institutions work identically or that a corporate federation possesses state sovereignty.

## Principles
- Employee representatives receive a mandate from employees, not appointments by their managers.
- Member delegates receive a mandate from their respective member companies under published internal procedures.
- No company can replace an employee representative merely for voting against company leadership.
- Common labor and human-rights baselines cannot be waived by member votes.
- Conflicts, recusals, minority opinions, legislative records and amendments are preserved.
- Shared powers must be expressly delegated by the founding charter; residual operational powers remain with members.
- Independent judicial review is institutionally separate from legislative and executive bodies.

## Not yet settled
Seat allocation, franchise, candidacy, voting method, election cycles, quorum, legislative thresholds, veto scope, executive appointment, tribunal jurisdiction, treaty amendment and withdrawal procedures. No such rule should be inferred from this decision.
