# FED-001: Federated enterprise and dogfooding

**Status:** Proposed working fictional canon, pending detailed governance decisions.
**Date:** 2026-11-12

## Vision
ZeroCloud is not one monolithic fictional corporation. It is a federation of small, semi-autonomous fictional operating companies, sharing a common charter, technical standards, fundamental employee protections and independently reviewable governance.

No fictional entity is represented as legally incorporated. Real implementations are separate from in-universe narrative.

## Candidate companies
- ZeroCloud Compute: cloud platform and worker fleet.
- ZeroCloud Security: SOC, security engineering and conformance.
- ZeroCloud Systems: internal open-source IT, collaboration and identity.
- ZeroCloud People: HR administration and independent employee protections.
- ZeroCloud Research: hardware qualification and technical research.
- ZeroCloud Business Services: procurement, accounting and shared services.

Names and legal/organizational boundaries remain proposals.

## Dogfooding mandate
Every internally developed service must identify:
1. An internal customer and documented use case.
2. A deployable open-source-first implementation or a clearly marked unimplemented design.
3. Interface and service-level contracts, operational owner and dependencies.
4. Tests and evidence, including failure modes.
5. A staged plan to use ZeroCloud compute when qualified.
6. A fallback and recovery procedure, avoiding circular bootstrap dependencies.

Dogfooding must never be used to conceal the absence of real deployments. Simulated internal customers and fictional invoices are labeled as such.

## Federation constraints
Small teams have autonomy within common security baselines, open-door access, pay-choice proposals, independent manager review, and legal obligations. Intercompany contracts cannot waive these rights. Financial transfers and service charges are fictional until explicitly implemented in a sandbox.

## Business agents
Agents may prepare procurement requests, risk assessments, budgets, service tickets and policy analyses. Require clear identity, least-privilege permissions, audit trails, review/approval gates and no invented human authorizations. No real-world payment or external communication without explicit approval.

## Dependencies
Preserve ZeroCloud architecture decisions 001-007, pending 008, security 009-D, HR proposals, and leadership LD-001-D. Two-pizza teams remain a candidate operating model, not an excuse to evade oversight.
