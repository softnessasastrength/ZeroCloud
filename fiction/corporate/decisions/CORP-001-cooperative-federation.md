# CORP-001: Cooperative federation

**Status:** Accepted as working fictional canon
**Choice:** B, cooperative federation
**Date:** 2026-11-12

## Decision
ZeroCloud is a fictional federation of autonomous member companies. Members jointly govern shared infrastructure, interoperability standards and baseline institutional protections. No single holding company unilaterally owns or directs every member. These are fictional operating entities, not claims of legal incorporation or actual cooperative status.

## Rationale
Preserve small-team autonomy and distinct institutional voices while giving members enforceable shared obligations. Member companies can disagree, contract, exit, and appeal. The federation is a common-good institution rather than a corporate command hierarchy.

## Constitutional baseline
- Member autonomy over internal operations within common technical, security and employee-rights baselines.
- Equal access to shared standards, documented service interfaces and dispute resolution.
- Meaningful open-door channels, independent manager review and paper-paycheck choice remain working HR proposals; detailed HR constitution is pending.
- Transparent member votes, recusal for conflicts, recorded minority opinions and no secret veto.
- No unapproved production security claims or fabricated organizational/legal status.
- Leadership Decision LD-001-D permits different governance experiments subject to nonwaivable rights.

## Decisions left open
Membership admission and exit criteria; vote allocation; dues and cost sharing; who may veto an unsafe deployment; final binding authority of the federation tribunal; actual legal structure (if ever implemented). Do not silently infer one-member-one-vote or unanimity.
