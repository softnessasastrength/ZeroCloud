# ZeroCloud Union: provisional constitutional blueprint

**Status:** Proposed constitutional design implementing CORP-002. Fictional, not an incorporated entity.

## Institutions
### Union Parliament
Direct employee representation across member companies. Proposed powers: scrutinize executive administration, debate employee-impacting legislation, approve shared policies alongside the Council, demand records with privacy safeguards, and refer constitutional disputes. Details of elections and representation remain open.

### Council of Members
Delegates of member companies. Proposed powers: review legislation affecting autonomy, approve intermember standards and common budgets, negotiate membership and shared-service rules. Voting formula and veto conditions remain open.

### Union Commission
Proposed small executive and administrative service for drafting policy, maintaining registries, implementing enacted rules and reporting compliance. It cannot unilaterally amend the charter or adjudicate complaints about itself.

### Union Court
Proposed independent adjudicative body for charter interpretation, conflicts between members and Union institutions, and appeals expressly granted by law. Judge selection, jurisdiction, binding remedies, independence and recusal are not yet decided.

### Independent Workplace Review
Employees retain direct access to workplace complaint and review processes without needing a parliamentary sponsor. Appeals to the Union Court require explicit jurisdictional rules; no automatic court route is implied.

## Legislative lifecycle (provisional)
1. Publish a proposal with legal basis, affected members, cost, security and employee-rights impact.
2. Seek feedback from both chambers and relevant independent bodies.
3. Record proposed amendments and minority opinions.
4. Obtain approvals under future dual-mandate voting rules.
5. Check consistency with charter and nonwaivable baseline.
6. Publish adopted text, effective date, implementation owner and appeal pathway.
7. Conduct periodic review and record outcomes.

## Competence boundaries
- Exclusive member powers: local delivery practices and operations not delegated to the Union.
- Shared powers (proposed): interoperable APIs, security minima, internal procurement standards and service portability.
- Protected baseline (proposed): anti-retaliation, accessible reporting, independent review, privacy and payment-method choice subject to applicable law.
- Reserved constitutional questions: membership, election rules, budgets, enforcement and amendments until adopted through an explicit charter process.

## Institutional safeguards
Separate legislative, administrative, investigative and adjudicative roles. Publish conflict disclosures, document emergency measures with expiration and review, require data minimization, and prohibit fictional business agents from impersonating elected officials or voting.

## Constitutional tension
The Council can object to cost and member autonomy; Parliament can object to employee impact. Neither disagreement automatically proves bad faith. The Union Court can address only matters within jurisdiction formally granted.
