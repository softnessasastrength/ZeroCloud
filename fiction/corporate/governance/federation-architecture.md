# Cooperative federation: organizational architecture

**Status:** Proposed detail under accepted CORP-001.

## Candidate member companies
- ZeroCloud Compute: compute and orchestration.
- ZeroCloud Security: security engineering, audit and incident response.
- ZeroCloud Systems: internal IT, identity and collaboration.
- ZeroCloud People: workplace services and employee advocacy.
- ZeroCloud Research: hardware certification and standards.
- ZeroCloud Business Services: procurement, accounting and intercompany service administration.

These names are placeholders. Each member maintains a charter, operating budget, accountable officers, independent internal review channels and versioned documentation.

## Joint institutions
- Federation Assembly: deliberates shared standards, membership and shared budgets; voting formula not yet selected.
- Technical Standards Council: API compatibility, K3s profiles, certification and change proposals.
- Security Council: minimum security controls, coordinated disclosure and emergency containment; scope of veto pending.
- Independent Review Office: handles intermember conflicts and appeals; cannot be staffed solely by the disputing member.
- Shared Services Registry: publishes service contracts, dependencies, service maturity and cost methodology.
- Archive and Editorial Office: labels fictional artifacts, preserves contradictory voices and records canon changes.

## Decision lifecycle
proposal -> published impact analysis -> comment and minority opinion -> conflict disclosure -> member decision under future voting rules -> appeal window -> versioned implementation -> periodic review.

## Exit and failure
Members must be able to export their data and migrate workloads through documented interfaces. Exit may not strand payroll, access to complaint channels or essential shared security functions. Plan contingency governance if a member goes offline.

## Narrative engine
A federation is not harmonious by definition. Every shared decision can generate a service provider memorandum, member objection, minority opinion, independent ruling and amended precedent.
