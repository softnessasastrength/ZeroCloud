# Dual representation: unresolved election design

**Status:** Deliberately open. CORP-002 decides who is represented, not how elections work.

## Parliament
- Who counts as an employee: full-time, part-time, temporary, contractors, employees of shared services?
- Is representation proportional, equal by company, or mixed?
- Secret ballot, nomination, campaigning and anti-coercion protections?
- How to avoid one large member dominating small members?
- Recall, term limits, vacancies and protected time for representative duties?

## Council
- Who appoints or elects each company's delegate?
- One company/one vote, weighted votes, or double-majority?
- What can delegates commit their company to?
- How are conflicts and abstentions treated?

## Lawmaking and checks
- What requires both chambers?
- Are there narrow exclusive powers?
- Who proposes bills, resolves deadlocks and can veto?
- Can either chamber request judicial review?
- What happens when no budget passes?

## Agent participation
Agents may summarize testimony and draft amendments with provenance; they cannot vote, count themselves as employees, certify elections or secretly influence ballots.
