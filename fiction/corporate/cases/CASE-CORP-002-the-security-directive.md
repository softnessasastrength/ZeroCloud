# CASE-CORP-002: The security directive

**Status:** Original fictional stress test; no canonical result selected.

## Facts
ZeroCloud Security proposes a federation-wide security directive requiring all member companies to migrate internal authentication to a shared identity platform within 60 days. Compute argues the deadline creates outage risk. Systems estimates the integration is possible but under-tested. Business Services says the project would displace planned spending. People warns that a rushed migration may lock out employees using accessibility accommodations.

## Dual mandates
- The **Council of Members** weighs member autonomy, cost, continuity and service obligations.
- The **Union Parliament** weighs employee access, privacy, safety and operational disruption.
- The **Commission** prepares an impact assessment and proposed text, without claiming legislative authority.
- The **Union Court**, if granted jurisdiction, considers whether the directive exceeds delegated competence or violates protected rights.

## Document sequence
1. Commission proposal and legal-basis memo
2. Security Council threat model and evidence limits
3. Compute member objection
4. Parliament employee-accessibility committee report
5. Council negotiation minutes
6. Amendment package and minority opinions
7. Deadlock memorandum or adopted directive
8. Possible judicial petition and reasoned disposition
9. Retrospective showing policy effects and institutional precedent

## Branches to explore
- A carefully limited directive passes with exceptions and independent verification.
- Both chambers deadlock; an interim voluntary standard is published.
- The directive passes but is challenged as beyond delegated powers.
- A security incident changes the evidence but does not erase due process.

## Guardrails
Do not invent election rules or voting thresholds. Do not assert any court jurisdiction or binding ruling before its constitutional grant. No branch is canon until separately decided. Fictional reports must not masquerade as real security certifications.
